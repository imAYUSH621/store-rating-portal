# PC MICROPHONE BOOST & TESTING GUIDE (AUDIO FIX)
**Priority:** Mandatory before 28th Oct 3:00 PM (Interview instructions require clear audio with headset).

---

## 🔊 METHOD 1: BOOST PC MICROPHONE IN WINDOWS SETTINGS (2 MINUTES)

Agar laptop/PC ka mic volume kam hai:

1. Press `Win + R`, type:
   ```text
   mmsys.cpl
   ```
   and press Enter (Sound Control Panel khul jayega).
2. Go to the **Recording (रिकॉर्डिंग)** tab.
3. Click on your active **Microphone** $\to$ click **Properties (प्रॉपर्टीज)**.
4. Go to the **Levels (लेवल्स)** tab:
   * **Microphone Slider:** Set to **90 – 100**.
   * **Microphone Boost Slider:** Set to **+10.0 dB** or **+20.0 dB** (agar awaz bohot faint hai toh +20 dB karo; +30 dB mat karna, background hiss aayega).
5. Go to **Advanced (एडवांस्ड)** tab:
   * Default Format: Select `2 channel, 16 bit, 48000 Hz (DVD Quality)` or `24 bit, 48000 Hz (Studio Quality)`.
   * Uncheck *"Enable audio enhancements"* if your voice sounds muffled or robotic.
6. Click **Apply** $\to$ **OK**.

## 📱 METHOD 2: THE PRO HACK — USE YOUR SMARTPHONE AS PC MIC (BEST QUALITY)
Laptop ke internal mic aksar bohot low-quality hote hain. Aapka mobile phone ek ultra-crisp studio mic ban sakta hai.

### Option A: Wired Phone Earphones / Headset (Fastest & Easiest)
* Mobile ke sath jo wired 3.5mm earphones aate hain (with mic button), unhe laptop ke 3.5mm audio jack me lagao.
* Earphones ka mic muh ke paas rehta hai, background noise 80% kam ho jati hai aur voice crystal clear aati hai.

### Option B: WO Mic (Turn Smartphone into USB Mic)
1. Phone pe Play Store se **WO Mic** install karo.
2. PC pe `wolicheng.com/womic` se **WO Mic Client + Driver** install karo.
3. Phone ko USB cable se PC me jodo (USB Debugging ON).
4. WO Mic Client me `USB` select karke **Connect** dabao.
5. Google Meet / Zoom me Microphone me **"WO Mic Device"** select karo. Voice studio quality ho jayegi!

## 🧪 METHOD 3: TESTING YOUR AUDIO BEFORE THE INTERVIEW

### Test 1: Windows Built-in Voice Recorder (Offline Test)
1. Start menu me type karo **"Voice Recorder"** (ya "Sound Recorder").
2. Record button dabao aur bolo:  
   *"Hello, this is Ayush Anand testing the microphone for Roxiler Systems interview."*
3. Playback suno:
   * Kya awaz loud aur clear hai?
   * Kya fan ya background noise jyada toh nahi?

### Test 2: Google Meet Self-Check (Exact Interview Environment)
1. Browser me jao: **`https://meet.google.com/new`**
2. Join karne se pehle **"Check your audio and video"** button pe click karo.
3. 5-second video clip record karo.
4. Listen to the playback. Google Meet show karega:
   * *Microphone quality: Good / Poor*
   * *Background noise level*

### Test 3: Online Mic Tester
* Open browser: **`https://webcammictest.com/check-mic.html`**
* Speak normally. The green wave bar should easily hit 70%–80% height.
